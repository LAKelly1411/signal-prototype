Signals → PA design system: contact sheet

Every component in signals/web/components, drawn with the PA design system (v0.17.0) so you can refine it before it's componentised. Where the DS already has the component, the real DS class is used. Where it doesn't, a draft pa- recipe below binds tokens only. Each entry is numbered, so you can say "change 7".

Reuse use the existing DS component as-is Extend add a variant or part to a DS component New a new DS component (draft recipe here) Out of scope
  1. Charts stay cobalt, which breaks a DS rule. Decision 0003 says "no cobalt on a chart mark" because cobalt means clickable. The relevance ring, activity pill, sparklines, weekly line, heat map and theme bars are drawn in cobalt by choice. Taking this into the DS needs 0003 changed first. The proposal adds dataviz.mark (Cobalt) and dataviz.seq-1…5 (a cobalt tint up to Tertiary Blue), so the charts no longer use hand-picked hex values.
  2. Depth: soft shadows and no borders, as the original prototype, which changes a DS rule. Cards are white on a white page with no hairline, and carry a wide soft shadow (proposed elevation.card, Ink 10%). On hover they lift 2px to elevation.card-hover (Ink 14%). Decision 0002 says every surface keeps its border and resting cards are flat, so it needs a rule change and two new tokens.
  3. Bold Press Sans at small sizes. Source names stay Regular, but labels, the meter word and table headers are Press Sans Bold at 15–16px, as today. The DS allows Bold Press Sans only from 24px, and sets small labels in condensed caps.
  4. Headings condensed; everything else Press Sans, no serif. Headings (page and section titles, card headlines, figures) stay Press Sans Condensed Bold, as the DS does. Labels, table headers and body text are Press Sans: Bold for labels in sentence case, Regular for body. The DS sets labels in condensed caps and editorial body text in the Frame Text serif, so that part is a signals change.
  5. Icons become Material Symbols Sharp 600 through pa-icon, in place of the mixed inline paths in Glyph.tsx and Icon.tsx.
  6. No orange tag, no green strip. The DS dropped the coloured "accent" tag (decision 0007) and has no success strip by design. Both are shown below as options for you to decide.

A · Cards

1

Signal card

SignalCard.tsx + card.module.css → product/signal-card

New
  • Why new: feed-item is a wire story with a time column and slug line. A signal has a source, a score and entities, which is a different anatomy. Could be a feed-item variant if you'd rather keep one feed component.
  • Changes: the original soft shadow and 2px hover lift, kept as proposed tokens elevation.card and card-hover; source name Regular (was Bold 16); headline Condensed Bold 28 (one step up from app-headline's 24; was Press Sans Bold 24); body stays Press Sans; "Source" becomes a DS ghost compact button; the exact-time tooltip moves to #18.
  • Decide: should the "part of a pattern" line link to the pattern?

Extend
